Ada Lovelace

PhD Candidate, Department of Computer Science
Analytical University

Email · Scholar · GitHub · CV

I am a PhD candidate at Analytical University, where I study the foundations of computation and the design of algorithms for symbolic machines. My work sits at the intersection of mathematics and mechanical reasoning, with a particular interest in how general purpose engines can manipulate not just numbers but abstract symbols.

Before my doctorate I worked on the theory of the Analytical Engine, where I wrote what is often regarded as the first algorithm intended to be carried out by a machine. Today I am broadly interested in the questions below. Feel free to reach out by email.

Research Interests

  • Algorithm design and analysis
  • Symbolic computation and computer algebra
  • Foundations of programmable machines
  • History and philosophy of computing
